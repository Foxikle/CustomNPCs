## Contribution Guidelines
Hi! I'm so happy you're looking to contribute to CustomNPCs!

This plugin started out as a project to get familiar with java, and I'm thrilled you think it is worth contributing to. 
I'm always open to hearing your ideas. But I do have a few requests of contributors:

- No Fully AI Generated "slop". It's fine if you use AI to write the code, but if you can't be bothered to read and understand it yourself, I won't either. CustomNPCs is written for humans, by humans.
- Document your rational. Don't just open a PR with no comment. I will read the changes, but I want to hear what you intended to change, and why. 
- Contributions are made under the MIT license. Any code that gets merged into the repository is governed by that license
- If you're planning on making a big change, or something you're uncertain will get approved, ask first! Save yourself the weekend of development.
- Please test your changes. Make sure the feature you wrote actually works how you intended it.

Thank you again for taking the time to read these short guidlines. Here's a cookie for your time <3 🍪

Cheers!
